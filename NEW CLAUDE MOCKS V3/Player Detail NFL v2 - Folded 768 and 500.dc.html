4Item 1 · Player detail, folded — 768 and 500

The four decisions

  1. The market tab row scrolls; Filters leaves it. At 768 the tabs keep the desktop composition — a strip on the left, Filters on the right — but the strip scrolls horizontally under a fade instead of wrapping. At 500 that pairing cramps both, so Filters drops to its own 44px row beneath the tabs and carries its count as a word. Same answer as the mobile handoff at 390, arrived at one width earlier.
  2. The verdict row becomes a card, not a wrap. Rate and sentence on the first line, then Average / Line / Clears it by as three equal divided cells — the same 1px rule the context row uses. The support pill sits with them rather than trailing the sentence. At 500 the sentence moves under the rate and the three cells hold their 3-up rhythm.
  3. The axis keeps the date while the column can hold it. Not a breakpoint — a rule: the date renders when a slot is at least 44px wide, which is true at 768 across ten slots and false at 500. The opponent crest and abbreviation are always there. Below the threshold the date lives in the log table, which is now on the page rather than only in a hover title.
  4. A seventeen-game log stops scrolling sideways. The graph is ten fixed slots at every width, so the fold makes the window control do the work: under 1100 the default lands on the largest stop that fits, All still selectable, and the graph states "last 10 of 17" with the rest in the log table below. A slot grid that scrolls is a grid that has given up on being a grid.

Held from the desktop file

The Reading the graph key survives the rail's removal, folded into a horizontal legend under the chart. Rails drop; MobilePlayerNav pins to the bottom with its 52×52 Lineup button and scrolling switcher, and the shell reserves 84px for it. Every rate states its sample; the graph, the verdict row and the log table read one array.

4a768 — iPad landscape, 13" laptop, a third of a 4K screen. Tabs scroll, Filters holds its corner.
