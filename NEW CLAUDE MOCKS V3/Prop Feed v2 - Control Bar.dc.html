5Item 2 · The Prop Feed's control bar

The problem, stated plainly

Seventeen equal pills is 17 equal claims on the eye, permanently, above a table whose whole job is to be scanned. The reversal is right — these are navigation — but navigation earns hierarchy, and right now Pass Yds and XP Made are the same size, weight and colour.

Three answers to the same question

5a collapses to one line. The row is a shelf of at most six markets, and you choose what fills it — star your own six, let usage order it, or take it alphabetically. Six is the cap because six 10.5px pills plus the label and the +more count fit one 32px line at 1040px and still fit at 768; a seventh wraps, and a row that can wrap is the thing we are fixing. Smallest permanent footprint; costs a click to reach XP Made.
5b groups by section. Passing / Rushing / Receiving already exist in the data, so the row becomes three labelled runs on one line, scrolling. Hierarchy comes free from the grouping; nothing is a click away.
5c promotes a section, demotes the rest. One section is open at a time as a segmented control; its markets sit beneath in full. The strongest hierarchy and the biggest behaviour change.

The division to preserve

State above, controls below — the summary strip says what you are looking at, the bar changes it. All three keep that, and none of them restates a control the bar already shows. Filters keeps the set-and-forget half.
