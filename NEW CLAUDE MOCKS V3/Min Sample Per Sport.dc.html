8Item 5 · Minimum sample, per sport

The tick scale is the problem, not the per-sport maximum

Eighteen bars for eighty games means each bar is 4.4 games and no bar is a step you can land on. The label under the cursor is doing all the work, which is why it feels like a lie — the scale asserts a granularity the control does not have.

Two honest ways out. 8a drops the per-game tick entirely and draws the scale as the thing it actually is: a continuous track with named stops, the notched slider the player pages already use for season length. 8b keeps ticks and makes them true — one tick per selectable value, which is the preset list plus All, so a five-tick NFL scale and a five-tick MLB scale both mean exactly what they show.

A value saved above the new sport's max

It is never discarded, and it is never silently rewritten. Each sport remembers its own last value, so leaving MLB at 50+ and returning finds 50+ waiting. Only a first visit to a sport inherits anything, and when the inherited number cannot stand there it is clamped to the nearest stop and the control says why — which is two different sentences, because there are two different reasons. 50+ on a 17-game season is out of reach; 17+ on a 44-game season is merely not one of its stops, and the notice names them. Try both: MLB 50+ → NFL, and NFL Full → WNBA.

On the chip row changing per sport

It should, and it should be visible that it did. Both options label the row with the season length it is scaled to — of 162 games — so a chip set that changes under you is explained by the thing next to it rather than surprising. Nothing else in the panel is per-sport because nothing else in the panel is measured in games.
