TURN 1

Mobile pass — Board, Prop Feed, Player Detail

430 × 932 · iPhone 15 Pro Max · tappable
DIAGNOSIS What the screenshots show
THE BOARD
Six filter groups before the first game. League, Sort games by, Show, Market, Minimum sample, Split — roughly 1,400px of controls above any content. On a 430px phone that is three full screens of chrome.
The market picker is a fixed-height vertical scrollbox inside a vertically scrolling page. Two vertical scrolls compete for the same drag; the inner one wins and the page stops moving. This is the mess you described.
Game card headers clip. The three-column away / time / home grid is a desktop width forced into 430px: "Cowboys" becomes "C…", "Giants" becomes "Gi…", and prop text cuts off mid-word — "OVER 9.5 RUSH A".
The My Picks button parks on top of content. It covers the bottom-right of whatever card is in view, including the "+17 players" row.
PROP FEED
Refine holds eleven control groups and eight paragraphs of explanation. Stat, Screens, Over/Under, window, main/alt lines, minimum sample plus a slider, defence tiers, role, lineups, sort, odds range. It is taller than three screens and every group carries a prose footnote.
Sort by is at the bottom of it. The single control people reach for most often is the one they have to scroll furthest to find.
The sheet doesn't read as a sheet. The nav row and league pills stay lit above it, so it looks like a section of the page rather than a modal — and the pill row behind it is cut in half.
WHAT CHANGES IN THESE MOCKS
Fixed compact header: 44px identity bar, one scrollable tab row, nothing else. It never partially scrolls.
One sticky control bar per screen. League, market and sort are dropdowns on it — visible, never inside a sheet.
No vertical scroll inside a vertical scroll, anywhere. Market selection is a dropdown panel that fits without scrolling.
Refine keeps four controls; everything else moves behind Advanced. Prose footnotes become one-line captions.
Feed rows are one line with the numbers that decide the tap; tap expands in place. The game-log bars are a horizontal strip you can drag, as on desktop.
