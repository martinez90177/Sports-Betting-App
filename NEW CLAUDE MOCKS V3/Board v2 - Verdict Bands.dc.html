7Item 4 · The verdict bands, five states

Grade them — but not with colour, and not with weight

Green and red are spoken for by the bars in the same row, so the pill cannot use hue. Weight is the obvious next reach and it is the wrong one: heavier type on Strong over makes it louder, which is a different claim than better supported. Loudness is an editorial voice; this product does not have one.

What the bands actually encode is how much of the pill the evidence has earned. So grade the pill's own fill: a 4-step accent-dim ramp read left to right, like a meter that happens to be pill-shaped. Not established is an empty outline; Strong over is nearly full. Same hue, same weight, same size — the difference is quantity, which is what the bands are.

Two readings of that idea

7a fills the pill itself. 7b keeps the pill flat and puts the ramp in a three-segment rail beneath the words, which is the device the player pages already use for TheRead — so the Board would borrow a component instead of inventing one. Both draw all five states, and 7c puts the winner on real Board cards.
