6Item 3 · The Role control

Two problems, one cause

The unit changes per sport and the thresholds live inside the pill text. Both come from the same decision — the pills are trying to say three things at once (the tier, the number, the unit), so the section title has nothing left to do and the prose line has to apologise for all of it.

The fix in both options

Move the unit out of the pills and into the section header, where it belongs — it is a property of the sport, not of the tier. The pills then carry a tier name and a bare number, matching every other pill row in the app. The prose line shrinks to one clause because the header is doing its share.

6a keeps the Defence section's shape exactly — micro-label, pill row, one line. 6b spends one extra row on a distribution strip, which is the honest way to say "12+ touches" means something different in the NBA than the NFL. Switch sport on either to see the unit change.

What neither does

Neither implies popularity. The word is role throughout, the sub-line says what it measures, and there is no mention of betting volume anywhere in the control — because there is no wagering feed and there never will be.
