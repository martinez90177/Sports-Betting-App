separate file · keep or discard
WHAT CHANGED FROM V4
A numbered rank implied precision it does not have. Nothing counted makes game #2 better than game #3. Tiers say the true thing instead: this one is worth ten minutes, these have one thing each, these have nothing.
The top game now looks like the top game. One hero card with its props and their form strips, rather than five cards of identical weight that make you read to find the difference.
The page carries the app's signature. Every prop shows the same bar strip the feed and player pages draw — green cleared, red outline missed, dashed line at the number — so the Board is recognisably part of the app rather than a list of chips.
A reason bar you can scan without reading. Each game's reasons are also a small stacked bar, so the shape of a slate is legible at a glance and the words are there when you want them.
